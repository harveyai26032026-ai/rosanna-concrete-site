About Rosanna Concrete & Landscaping

Founded in 2014, we are a family‑owned Melbourne concrete contractor with more than a decade of experience delivering high‑quality concrete paving, driveways, retaining walls and garden landscaping across Rosanna and the surrounding suburbs.

Our Story

What started as a small crew of skilled tradespeople has grown into a trusted team known for punctuality, affordability and an uncompromising focus on quality. We work closely with local builders, councils and suppliers to ensure every project – big or small – meets the highest standards.
